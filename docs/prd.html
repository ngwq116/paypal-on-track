Product Requirements Document

On-Track

An AI payment desk for marketing agencies: collect from clients, pay influencers when posts go live, pay freelancers against signed contracts. Everything is shown on one shared timeline and calendar.

Author: ngwanqin Date: Oct 12, 2026 Status: Draft Ships: Nov 12, 2026 (hackathon deadline)

1. Problem

A marketing agency sits in the middle of every campaign's money. It collects fees from the client, pays a roster of influencers and KOLs once their posts go live, and pays freelancers whose amounts were fixed when the contract was signed. Today that is tracked across spreadsheets, chat threads, screenshots and bank transfers.

That causes three expensive failures:

  • Paying out before getting paid. Creators are paid while the client deposit is still outstanding, so the agency carries the cash risk.
  • Slow, unpredictable creator payments. Someone has to check every post (brand tagged, hashtags, posting date) by hand and chase approvals before releasing payment, so creators wait weeks.
  • Invoice mismatches slipping through. Freelancer invoices that differ from the contracted amount are only caught if someone cross-checks the contract.

Evidence (online research, Oct 12, 2026)

Clients on net 30–120 terms often take about 65 days to pay; large companies keep moving toward 120+ day terms.IZEA

Existing tools pay creators for brands, but none protects the agency in the middle. See market research.

2. Users

On-Track is an internal tool for the agency only. Every user works at the agency.

Mei, finance & ops lead

Runs 4–6 campaigns at once for a small Kuala Lumpur agency. At month end she reconciles client payments against 20+ creator payouts in a spreadsheet, and collects post screenshots and invoices from WhatsApp and email. Primary user.

Arif, account manager

Owns the client relationship and the campaign calendar. Needs to see at a glance which posts are due this week and whether a late post will delay the client's final invoice.

Hana, agency director

Signs off payments. Wants one approval queue showing what is about to be paid, to whom, why, and whether the client has paid, so she can approve in batches.

Who is not a user

These parties never log into On-Track. The agency acts on their behalf, and they only see the PayPal side.

PartyRelationshipWhat they see
Influencer / KOLPayeeSends post proof to the agency as they do today (WhatsApp, email). Receives a PayPal payout.
FreelancerPayeeSends an invoice to the agency as they do today. Receives a PayPal payout.
Client (brand)PayerReceives PayPal invoices and pays them.

3. Goals

  • G1. Never pay out money the agency hasn't received. Creator payouts on a campaign are locked until the client's deposit clears.
  • G2. Pay creators on a clear schedule as soon as work is verified and approved, not 60–90 days later.
  • G3. Catch invoice and deliverable problems automatically, before money moves.
  • G4. One shared picture of the campaign: who owes what, who is owed what, and when, in both a team timeline and a calendar.
  • G5. Win the hackathon. A working, runnable (setup steps in the README), end-to-end demo with a clear video.

5. Non-goals (this release)

  • Creator discovery or matching from the influencer pool. The roster is seed data.
  • Fully automatic post verification. A person confirms every post (R8). AI only pre-fills the checklist (R24).
  • Fetching posts from Instagram or TikTok APIs. Proof is a screenshot or link that agency staff upload.
  • Multi-agency accounts, login and roles. One agency, one demo user.
  • Multiple currencies, FX, tax or withholding. USD only (see Q4).
  • Contract drafting or e-signing. On-Track reads an already-signed contract.
  • Portals or logins for creators, freelancers or clients. On-Track is agency-only. Outside parties interact only through PayPal payouts and invoices.
  • Gantt view and mobile app.

Guardrails, agent and audit

IDRequirementPriority
R15No payout is sent on a campaign whose client deposit has not cleared. This is enforced in code, not left to the AI.Must
R16No payout may exceed the contracted fee for that assignment, and an assignment can be paid only once.Must
R17Every payout needs approval by a person (Mei or Hana) before it is scheduled. The approval queue shows payee, amount, contract fee, verification result and client deposit status. Approvals can be done one by one or in a batch.Must
R25An approved payout appears as a scheduled block on its payout date (from the contract) in both views and is sent through PayPal automatically on that date. Staff can also choose Send now.Must
R26Staff can edit a payout's amount and payout date any time before it is sent. The amount can be lowered but never raised above the contract fee (raising it needs an amended contract). The date can't be in the past. A reason is required and saved to the audit log. Editing a payout that is already approved sends it back for approval. Sent payouts can't be edited.Must
R21Every AI verdict, approval, payout and invoice change is recorded in an audit log with who/what made it and why.Must
R22The user can give the agent a plain-language instruction such as "pay every creator with a verified post on this campaign". The agent shows its plan and the guardrail result for each item before executing.Should
R23The system suggests creators from the roster for a new brief.Later

8. Demo script (≤ 2:50)

  1. Contract in, timeline out (0:00–0:30). Upload a client contract. The AI extracts the terms, the user confirms, and both views fill with the campaign.
  2. Client pays (0:30–0:55). Send the deposit invoice and pay it in the PayPal sandbox. The webhook arrives and the payout lock opens on screen.
  3. Influencer verified and paid (0:55–1:25). Upload the post screenshot the creator sent and tick the checklist (or let the AI pre-check fill it, if built). The payout lands in the approval queue. Approve it: it appears on the calendar as scheduled. Click Send now: the PayPal payout goes out and the block turns green.
  4. Freelancer mismatch caught (1:25–1:55). A $1,200 invoice against a $1,000 contract is flagged and held. The corrected invoice is paid.
  5. Drag to reschedule (1:55–2:25). Drag a post from Tuesday to Friday in the Calendar. The agent moves the payout and proposes moving the client's balance invoice. Approve, and the PayPal invoice updates.
  6. Close on the numbers (2:25–2:50). Campaign summary: billed, received, paid out, margin, and the audit log.

9. Assumptions & dependencies

  • All money moves in the PayPal sandbox. Creators and the client are sandbox accounts.
  • The sandbox business account can use Payouts and Invoicing (verify in week 1, Q1).
  • Bryntum Scheduler and Calendar trial licences cover the build and judging period (Q2).
  • Claude Haiku 5.5 can read contract PDFs and invoice PDFs or images reliably enough for the seeded demo data. If it can't, switching to Claude Sonnet 5.5 is a one-setting change (Q7).
  • Contracts follow one template for the demo. Arbitrary contract formats are not required to work.

11. Open questions & decisions

#QuestionStatusAnswer / next step
Q1Can the sandbox business account send Payouts, and can sandbox personal accounts receive them?AnsweredYes. Proven by npm run paypal:smoke (batch 9XHJM27W45RQA) and by the approval queue (batch 9XGCCP7LQS328, paid via webhook). The sending account must hold a USD balance (a MYR-only account fails with INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS), the payee must be a different account (SELF_PAY_NOT_ALLOWED), and turning on an app feature (Invoicing) only applies after the cached access token is revoked or expires (up to 9 hours).
Q2Will Bryntum trial packages keep working for judges after the trial ends?Partly answeredBryntum 7.3.7 trial packages install from public npm with no registry login (@bryntum/scheduler@npm:@bryntum/scheduler-trial, same for calendar). Still to ask on the PayPal Discord: do trials keep working for judges after 45 days? A video alone does not meet the rules: judges must be able to run the project or open a hosted URL.
Q3Does the PayPal Agent Toolkit / MCP server include Payouts?AnsweredNo. The PayPal MCP server has orders, 7 invoice tools, subscriptions, disputes, catalog, shipment tracking and reporting, but no payouts. Payouts use the REST API directly (POST /v1/payments/payouts). Source: PayPal AI-Toolkit tool inventory.
Q4Demo currency?DecidedUSD.
Q5When does a payout need a human?DecidedAlways. Every payout is approved by a person before it is scheduled (R17). No auto-approve limit or confidence threshold.
Q6Is the problem real, and what are the baselines?Answered onlineYes. See the evidence table in section 1. Optional: one quote from a Malaysian agency or creator for the video.
Q7Which AI model?DecidedClaude Haiku 5.5. PDF and image input is supported (all active models). If it misses the G3 targets during the week 2 eval, switch AI_MODEL to Claude Sonnet 5.5.
