Competitive landscape

Market research

What already exists for paying influencers, creators and freelancers, and where On-Track is different.

Date: Oct 12, 2026 Method: web search + vendor pages Status: Draft

1. Summary

Similar products exist, but none combines what On-Track does. The existing tools show the problem is real (which helps the PRD's impact case). The gaps leave room for something new.

  • Influencer suites (Grin, Later, Meltwater, HypeAuditor, Heepsy) pay creators on behalf of brands. Verifying the post is still mostly manual.
  • Payout services (Creatorcheck, Trolley) move money but don't understand campaigns, contracts or deliverables.
  • Hackathon projects (AdLoom, FlowBack) already do "AI checks the post, then pays", but in crypto and without an agency in the middle.
  • Not found anywhere: locking creator payouts until the client has paid the agency, checking freelancer invoices against the contract, and a calendar linked to both sides of the money.

Most sources are vendor marketing pages, review roundups and hackathon showcases. Feature claims below are as stated by those sources and have not been tested hands-on.

4. Similar hackathon projects

These are the closest matches to On-Track's "AI checks the post, then pays" step.

AdLoom (ETHGlobal)

AI agents check posts for hashtags, mentions and campaign compliance, then release PYUSD from escrow. Claims under 30 s verification and a fixed 2.5% fee.

FlowBack (Chainlink hackathon)

Brands deposit USDC with payout rules (e.g. $10 per 1,000 views). Pulls YouTube/TikTok engagement, runs AI content checks, auto-releases payment.

What they lack: both are crypto and smart-contract based, both go brand → creator directly, and neither handles client billing, freelancer invoices or scheduling.

Industry context

  • Trade coverage (Influencers Time) describes deliverable verification as still largely manual: someone confirms the post went live, met the brief and stayed up for the required time.
  • The same outlet argues AI agents are starting to verify deliverables and trigger same-day payouts, which supports the timing of On-Track's idea.

5. Malaysia & Southeast Asia

  • No local KOL payment platform was found in this search. Results were mostly job postings.
  • KOLHUB (Malaysian influencer agency, founded 2020) shows no payment tooling in its public profile.
  • BJAK and Garena are hiring in-house KOL managers whose roles cover sourcing, contracts and reporting. This suggests KOL management, including payments, is often done by hand inside companies.

This is a weak signal from one search, not proof of an empty market. Follow-up searches to run: "influencer payout platform Malaysia", "KOL payment software Southeast Asia".

7. Positioning

"Influencer tools help brands pay creators. On-Track protects the agency in the middle: no creator is paid before the client pays, and every payment is checked against the contract."

  • Lead with cash-flow protection, not "AI verifies posts". Post verification alone has already been done (AdLoom, FlowBack).
  • Name the existing tools in the video in one sentence ("tools like Grin pay creators for brands…"). It shows judges you know the market and makes the gap obvious.
  • Use the market as evidence for impact: several funded products exist for creator payments, so the problem is real, but none serves the agency's two-sided cash flow.
  • Don't compete on breadth. KYC, tax and global coverage are what payout services do well. Keep them as non-goals.
