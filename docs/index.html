On-Track

Keep every campaign, creator and payment on track. An AI payment desk for marketing agencies that bill clients, pay influencers when their post goes live, and pay freelancers against signed contracts, all on one shared timeline and calendar.

Deadline: Nov 12, 2026 · 12:00pm PST Solo · ~35 hours Status: Draft

Product Requirements (PRD)

Problem, personas, goals, success metrics, non-goals, numbered requirements, demo script and open questions.

Market research

Existing influencer and payout tools, similar hackathon projects, feature comparison and positioning.

Tech stack & architecture

Stack choices, system diagram, data model, payment flows, AI design, guardrails, webhooks and the weekly build plan.

One-line pitch

Client money in, creator money out, nothing paid early. On-Track reads the contract, invoices the client through PayPal, checks each freelancer invoice against the contract with AI, gives staff a quick checklist for each influencer post, and only releases PayPal Payouts once the client has paid and the work is verified.
